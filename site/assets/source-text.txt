HI, I’M DR. ZIAH COLON 
Dr. Ziah M. Colon is a higher education executive, educator, consultant, pastor, and nonprofit leader dedicated to
advancing excellence in education, leadership development, and community engagement. She currently serves as the
Structured Teaching Assistant Program Coordinator at Morgan State University, where she leads and manages more than
140 graduate teaching assistants across multiple academic disciplines. In this role, she oversees program operations,
develops comprehensive training initiatives, and facilitates professional development programs that strengthen
instructional excellence, faculty support, and student success.In addition to her administrative leadership, Dr. Owens-Colon
serves as an Instructor of Record for English 101 and English 102 and teaches Women's and Gender Studies at the
university level. Her passion for teaching is centered on developing critical thinkers, fostering inclusive learning
environments, and preparing students for academic and professional success.
Dr. Colon is the President and CEO of The Colon Consulting Group, where she provides strategic consulting, leadership
development, higher education solutions, curriculum design, program assessment, professional training, and
organizational development services for colleges, universities, nonprofit organizations, and community partners. Her
expertise includes teaching assistant program development, faculty and staff training, student success initiatives, strategic
planning, leadership coaching, and organizational effectiveness.
She also serves as Pastor of The Holy Covenant Church of Christ, Inc., where she provides spiritual leadership, community
outreach, and faith-based educational programming. Her ministry emphasizes servant leadership, empowerment, and
holistic community transformation.
Dr. Owens-Colon holds a Doctor of Education in Community College Leadership, a Master of Science in Counseling with a
concentration in Addiction, and a strong commitment to interdisciplinary scholarship and service. Her research focuses on
Black maternal health and nursing, with particular emphasis on advancing health equity, improving maternal outcomes,
and addressing systemic disparities affecting Black women and families. Her academic and professional work is grounded
in evidence-based practice, equity-centered leadership, and institutional transformation.
A committed advocate for community advancement, Dr. Owens-Colon is the Founder and former President of I AM
Baltimore, a youth-focused nonprofit organization that provided educational enrichment, mentoring, leadership
development, and GED preparation programming for middle and high school students. She also serves as President of the
Robert L. Owens Foundation, an organization dedicated to expanding educational opportunities, supporting veterans, and
strengthening communities through outreach, advocacy, and service.
